Import sales from file (admin)

Bulk-add sales from the Excel template — for example copying quantities from your POS report.

Get the template, fill Date / Menu Item / Qty / Type (blank, OC or Ent), then Save As → “CSV UTF-8” or “XML Spreadsheet 2003” and import it here. Inventory forecast — next 4 days

Based on average daily consumption over the last 5 days — computed automatically from your sales and locked (no manual edits). Use the outlet buttons to forecast All outlets together or one outlet by itself — Stock now and Avg/day follow the pick. "Need 4 days" = how much you will use; "Suggest order" = how short you will be if you want 4 days covered.

Stock movement

Issue (Store) = stock from the main store (in). Pick the outlet the movement belongs to — it feeds that outlet's Stock Card. OC and Ent are recorded in Daily Sales with the OC / Ent tick.

Movement log

Movement totals per outlet and ingredient feed each outlet's Stock Card. Use the outlet buttons to see all movements or just one outlet's.

Stock card

Expected Closing = Opening + Purchase + TF-In + Issue (Store) − Sales − TF-Out − OC − Ent − Spoils. Sales = units consumed via Daily Sales (POS qty × recipe). Every outlet keeps its own card — pick it below to see and count its stock. "All outlets" adds every outlet together; the count column needs a single outlet. Press 🗓 Month end for the guided count — it checks that every ingredient of every outlet is counted before closing the month.

Item Sale — menu master

Ingredients — stock master

This app stores your data inside this browser on this computer. Click "Download backup" regularly (at least weekly and at month end) and keep the file — if the browser data is cleared, you can restore everything with "Load backup".

New month

Starts a fresh month, per outlet: last month's counts (or expected closing where not counted) become that outlet's new Opening, and sales / movements / counts are cleared. Download a backup first!

Closed months — history

Every finished month is kept here automatically — revenue, sales and movement counts, and the stock reconciliation per outlet. It is saved at the moment you do "Complete month end" or "Start new month", so past months stay viewable inside the app — no backup file is needed just to look back at a closed month.

Outlets — setup

Outlets are your selling points (bars / branches) — add one for every place you sell or hold stock (for example “WFH”). Each outlet keeps its own Stock Card: its own opening, movements and month-end count. Renaming moves its sales, movements and stock; deleting merges them into another outlet. Press ★ to make an outlet the default one the app opens on.
